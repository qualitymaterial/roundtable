# Community code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Discuss ideas and evidence; avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention. Respect consent and privacy. Do not publish another person's credentials or private information.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation, with responses proportionate to the conduct. Reports should be made privately to maintainers. A dedicated private reporting channel is not yet established; request private contact without publishing personal details. The source repository is https://github.com/qualitymaterial/roundtable.
